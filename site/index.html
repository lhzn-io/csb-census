Participating commercial and recreational vessels log depth and position with their own echosounders and GNSS receivers during routine operations. Under the IHO's Crowdsourced Bathymetry initiative, Trusted Nodes submit these soundings to the Data Centre for Digital Bathymetry (DCDB), and NOAA NCEI publishes them to an open archive in four batches a day. The archive is one of the sources for the Seabed 2030 global bathymetry.

This census reads each batch, identifies soundings published more than once, and counts published and unique soundings per cell of an H3 grid. It counts what was published; it does not assess accuracy. Individual soundings can be viewed in the DCDB data viewer.
