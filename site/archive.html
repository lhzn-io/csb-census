Long Island Sound

How it is counted

A sounding's identity is its position, depth and time exactly as DCDB publishes them. The first copy published is the original; later copies are duplicates, either resent under the same platform ID or repeated under another. A vessel-day is one platform ID in one cell on one day, and copies under another ID add none. Duplicates are measured in NCEI's published archive; causes are not attributed.

Independent census by Long Horizon Observatory from NOAA NCEI / IHO DCDB public data, also browsable in the DCDB data viewer. Not for navigation. Methodology · About · Code
